# 19 Campaign and Settlement mode (M6)

The full rules are in the Campaign Handbook (CH p4-6, p18-26), which has no free copy; the later
Campaign Book is paid. This file records only what free sources show, and marks the rest as GAP.

## 1. What free sources establish
- Card decks used by campaigns: Event, Explore, Quest, Perk, Boost cards; Danger, Creature, Stranger
  and Item cards form The Wasteland (RoP s1.4 p6; s3.2 p31). Events are drawn from The Wasteland during
  battles (RoP p31; CH p4).
- Perks (CH p5): bought per model, never twice for one model; settlement-wide perks (e.g. Gun Nut) only
  once per settlement (ERR p.10 FAQ). Perk caps costs exist (COST "PERKS").
- Boosts (CH p5): one-shot effects, e.g. reroll an X (Second Chance: the AP of an X+AP face is not
  kept), reroll remaining effect dice (Try That Again v2), negate one damage icon (ERR p.13-15, p.18).
- Quests (CH p6): multi-part quest cards; each completed part counts as 1 toward buying new land; two
  team players cannot run the same quest (ERR p.10; COOP s2).
- Battles award caps by scenario result (BOX; RR). A draw gives both players the winner's caps (ERR p.10).
- Settlement pool (CH p18-24): players own a pool of cards; after a battle unique items join the pool
  automatically unless the player chooses to sell them; other items need Maintenance Sheds to be kept;
  unselected cards are sold at the end of step 3 unless stored in Lockers (ERR p.3, p.15).
- Limited-use items refresh between battles; each copy usable once per game (ERR p.17).
- Explore cards are resolved by the Leader, who may take items from the pool, committed before the
  draw; food cannot prevent damage there (ERR p.10).
- Structures named by errata (cost in caps): Cooking Station (draw 1 Food & Drink/Alcohol per Crop
  Field, keep 1, max 5 draws), Robot Workbench 100 (needs Robotics Expert perk; 1 robot mod each),
  Lockers 70 (store 1 item each for a battle), Robot Market 250 (one per settlement; draw 4 robot mods,
  buy any), Ranger Outpost 100, Maintenance Shed, Crop Field, Scavenging Stand, small Boston Bugle
  magazine racks (ERR p.3, p.5).
- Settlement-mode force rules: AI units get standard equipment and ignore item minimums; Equipped
  items on unit cards are mandatory (ERR p.4, p.15).
- Settlement sheet v4.0 PDF exists on the 1st-ed downloads page (not yet fetched).
- Homestead Survival Mode (later expansion): no caps limit, no minimum structures, multiple
  addictions per distinct chem (ERR p.17). Out of scope.
