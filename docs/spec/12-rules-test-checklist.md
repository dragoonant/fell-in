# 12 Rules test checklist

One line = one testable rule. Engine tests are named by ID, e.g. `it('DICE-004 ...')`. Source keys in
`10`. Errata wins over RoP.

## TURN: rounds, readiness, advantage
- TURN-001: Every model starts each round Unready; all readiness markers clear at round end. (RoP s2.6 p13)
- TURN-002: A turn starts by making exactly one Unready model Ready; Ready/Used models are not eligible. (RoP p13)
- TURN-003: After readying, the player either Uses none or Uses all of their Ready models. (RoP p13)
- TURN-004: Using all is forced when every one of the player's models has a readiness marker. (RoP p13)
- TURN-005: Ready models Used in one turn may come from any units, in any order. (RoP p13)
- TURN-006: A model's activation completes fully before the next model starts; it cannot activate twice in a round. (RoP p13)
- TURN-007: Ready models not activated when a turn ends become Used. (RoP p13; ERR p.8)
- TURN-008: A player with all models Used takes no further turns; the opponent continues alone. (RoP p13)
- TURN-009: The round ends when all models on both sides are Used. (RoP p13)
- TURN-010: Round 1 Advantage goes to the side with fewer models, random on a tie, unless the scenario sets it. (RoP p13)
- TURN-011: From round 2, Advantage goes to the side with fewer models; on a tie the holder passes it. (RoP p13)
- TURN-012: The Advantage holder chooses which side takes the first turn of the round. (RoP p13)
- TURN-013: Default deployment alternates one model at a time from the Advantage holder. (ERR p.4)

## ACT: activation and actions
- ACT-001: A Used model may take up to two Actions, repeats allowed. (RoP s2.6 p14)
- ACT-002: An Action cannot start before the previous Action ends (no shooting mid-move). (RoP p14)
- ACT-003: Models have no facing. (RoP p14)
- ACT-004: Items may be used immediately before or after any Action, including reactions. (RoP s3.3 p33)
- ACT-005: A model without the skill icon for a test cannot attempt it. (RoP s2.4 p10)
- ACT-006: "Use" on a card needs an Action; "Test" needs none but is once per activation. (ERR p.9)
- ACT-007: Activation start discards the model's Reaction marker before anything else. (RoP s4.4 p41)

## AP: quick actions and action points
- AP-001: A Quick Action with a skill test has -2 SV. (RoP s4.3 p40)
- AP-002: A Quick Action that moves uses one ruler colour shorter. (RoP p40)
- AP-003: V.A.T.S. rolls one blue die at activation and grants 1 AP per bottle icon. (RoP p40; ERR p.2)
- AP-004: An AP face on the skill die grants 1 AP even when the test fails (incl. the X+AP face). (RoP p11)
- AP-005: AP are never gained during Quick Actions or reactions. (RoP p40-41)
- AP-006: Unspent AP are discarded at activation end and never transfer. (RoP p40)
- AP-007: 1 AP buys one Quick Action of an AP-use icon on the model's cards; each icon once per activation. (RoP p40)
- AP-008: A Shoot AP icon cannot be used for a ranged attack while engaged. (ERR p.15)

## DICE: dice faces and pools
