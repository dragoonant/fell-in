# 12 Rules test checklist

One line = one testable rule. Engine tests are named by ID, e.g. `it('DICE-004 ...')`. Source keys in
`10`. Errata wins over RoP.

## TURN: rounds, readiness, advantage
- TURN-001: Every model starts each round Unready; all readiness markers clear at round end. (RoP s2.6 p13)
- TURN-002: A turn starts by making exactly one Unready model Ready; Ready/Used models are not eligible. (RoP p13)
- TURN-003: After readying, the player either Uses none or Uses all of their Ready models. (RoP p13)
- TURN-004: Using all is forced when every one of the player's models has a readiness marker. (RoP p13)
- TURN-005: Ready models Used in one turn may come from any units, in any order. (RoP p13)
- TURN-006: A model's activation completes fully before the next model starts; it cannot activate twice in a round. (RoP p13)
- TURN-007: Ready models not activated when a turn ends become Used. (RoP p13; ERR p.8)
- TURN-008: A player with all models Used takes no further turns; the opponent continues alone. (RoP p13)
- TURN-009: The round ends when all models on both sides are Used. (RoP p13)
- TURN-010: Round 1 Advantage goes to the side with fewer models, random on a tie, unless the scenario sets it. (RoP p13)
- TURN-011: From round 2, Advantage goes to the side with fewer models; on a tie the holder passes it. (RoP p13)
- TURN-012: The Advantage holder chooses which side takes the first turn of the round. (RoP p13)
- TURN-013: Default deployment alternates one model at a time from the Advantage holder. (ERR p.4)

## ACT: activation and actions
- ACT-001: A Used model may take up to two Actions, repeats allowed. (RoP s2.6 p14)
- ACT-002: An Action cannot start before the previous Action ends (no shooting mid-move). (RoP p14)
- ACT-003: Models have no facing. (RoP p14)
- ACT-004: Items may be used immediately before or after any Action, including reactions. (RoP s3.3 p33)
- ACT-005: A model without the skill icon for a test cannot attempt it. (RoP s2.4 p10)
- ACT-006: "Use" on a card needs an Action; "Test" needs none but is once per activation. (ERR p.9)
- ACT-007: Activation start discards the model's Reaction marker before anything else. (RoP s4.4 p41)

## AP: quick actions and action points
- AP-001: A Quick Action with a skill test has -2 SV. (RoP s4.3 p40)
- AP-002: A Quick Action that moves uses one ruler colour shorter. (RoP p40)
- AP-003: V.A.T.S. rolls one blue die at activation and grants 1 AP per bottle icon. (RoP p40; ERR p.2)
- AP-004: An AP face on the skill die grants 1 AP even when the test fails (incl. the X+AP face). (RoP p11)
- AP-005: AP are never gained during Quick Actions or reactions. (RoP p40-41)
- AP-006: Unspent AP are discarded at activation end and never transfer. (RoP p40)
- AP-007: 1 AP buys one Quick Action of an AP-use icon on the model's cards; each icon once per activation. (RoP p40)
- AP-008: A Shoot AP icon cannot be used for a ranged attack while engaged. (ERR p.15)

## DICE: dice faces and pools
- DICE-001: Skill die has 2 AP faces, 3 CP faces, one each of 2,3,4,5, two each of 6,7,8, one each of 9,10, two X and one X+AP. (DICE)
- DICE-002: AP and CP faces count as a result of 1. (RoP p11)
- DICE-003: Black die: 5 DMG, 1 DMG+DMG, 3 blank, 1 BOT, 1 AR, 1 ACC-1. (DICE)
- DICE-004: Green die: 4 ACC-1, 2 ACC-2, 1 ACC-3, 2 AR, 2 DMG, 1 BOT. (DICE)
- DICE-005: Yellow die: 5 AR, 1 AR+AR, 2 blank, 1 BOT, 2 ACC-1, 1 DMG. (DICE)
- DICE-006: Blue die: 4 BOT, 2 BOT+BOT, 1 BOT+STAR, 2 STAR, 1 STAR+STAR, 2 BLAST. (DICE)
- DICE-007: Armor die: four 1s, three 2s, three 3s, two 4s. (DICE)
- DICE-008: At most 4 dice of one colour per test; excess dropped before rolling. (RoP p11)
- DICE-009: At most one bonus-die token per colour per test. (ERR p.9)
- DICE-010: Dice tables load from the edition data file, not engine literals. (20)

## SKILL: skill tests

- SKILL-002: All SV modifiers are summed before comparison. (RoP p10)
- SKILL-003: X always fails and cannot be modified. (RoP p10)
- SKILL-004: With adjusted SV below 1, only a CP face succeeds. (RoP p10)
- SKILL-005: On a failed test only skill-die AP icons count. (RoP p10)
- SKILL-006: Speciality gives +2 SV with its item/skill. (RoP p10)
- SKILL-007: Opposed test: higher successful result wins; a winner must succeed; tie to higher SV. (RoP p10; ERR p.9)
- SKILL-008: Choosing to fail sets the skill die to X before rolling; effect dice still roll. (ERR p.8)
- SKILL-009: Hit odds shown in the UI equal the exact enumerated probability. (13 s7)

## MOVE: movement and terrain
- MOVE-001: A straight Move displaces the base by at most colour + base diameter (front-to-rear rule). (RoP s2.7 p15)
- MOVE-002: Models cannot pass through opposing bases. (RoP p15)
- MOVE-003: Models may pass through friendly bases but not end overlapping. (RoP p15)
- MOVE-004: Crossing any difficult terrain makes that whole movement one colour shorter. (RoP s4.2 p37)
- MOVE-005: Unimpeded models ignore difficult terrain. (RoP p37)
- MOVE-006: Slow applies before other shortening; nothing goes below Orange. (RoP s5.4 p48; ERR p.9)
- MOVE-007: Climb start requires an Agi test; failure falls from the start point and ends the Action. (RoP p38; ERR p.2)
- MOVE-008: Falling damage = black die DMG icons + table (Orange 0 .. Black +10, beyond Black removed); BOT icons reduce armor. (RoP p39)
- MOVE-009: Power Armor negates all falling damage and removal. (RoP p39, p45)
- MOVE-010: A model forced over the table edge by an enemy stays on and takes 1 damage ignoring armor. (RoP p39; ERR p.2)
- MOVE-011: A model whose base crosses the edge by its own move leaves and scores as eliminated. (RoP p39)
- MOVE-012: Models may never end on a marker or token. (ERR p.8)
- MOVE-013: Jumping down resolves falling one colour lower; Orange or less is free. (RoP p38)

## RANGE: rulers and measuring
- RANGE-001: Ruler lengths are Orange 2, Yellow 4, Red 6, Green 8, Blue 10, Black 12 inches. (DICE)
- RANGE-002: One colour shorter of Orange is Orange. (ERR p.9)
- RANGE-003: A target is in range if the ruler reaches any part of its base. (ERR p.9)
- RANGE-004: Long range starts where short range ends (S..S+L). (RoP s2.8 p16)
- RANGE-005: A base straddling both bands lets the attacker choose the band. (RoP p16)
- RANGE-006: A weapon without a long band cannot hit beyond short. (RoP p16)
- RANGE-007: Thrown weapon range equals the thrower's Throw icon colour. (RoP s5.1 p44)

## LOS: line of sight
- LOS-001: LoS exists if any line from any point of one base to any point of the other is unblocked. (RoP p17)
- LOS-002: Normal and Large models do not block LoS. (RoP p17)
- LOS-003: Huge models block LoS for smaller models. (RoP p17)
- LOS-004: An elevated model more than Orange from a Significant Edge has LoS blocked across it. (RoP p18)
- LOS-005: Attack triggers do not need LoS; other triggers do. (ERR p.2)
- LOS-006: Charge requires LoS to the charged model at the start (unless Senses). (RoP p23; CARDS p.6)

## COVER: cover
- COVER-001: Each object fully crossed by the centre-to-centre LoC gives -2 SV. (RoP p17)
- COVER-002: Cover touched by the shooter's base is ignored. (RoP p17)
- COVER-003: Any cover adds +1 target armor once, regardless of count. (RoP p17)
- COVER-004: Area terrain gives cover when the LoC merely enters it. (RoP p18)
- COVER-005: Non-friendly models on the LoC give cover; friendly models do not. (RoP p17)
- COVER-006: Huge models give cover to smaller models except those engaged with them. (RoP p17)
- COVER-007: An elevated shooter counts only cover within Orange of the target. (RoP p18)
- COVER-008: A Significant Edge within Orange of an elevated target counts as cover. (RoP p18)
