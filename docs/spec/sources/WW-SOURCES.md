## 4. Mechanics overview (own words; source: Rules of Play + AI Handbook PDFs, cited by section)
- Round: every model activates once per round. Players alternate "turns"; on a turn you mark one unready model Ready, then either stop or Use ALL your Ready models in any order. Advantage marker (fewest models) picks who goes first. (Rules of Play s2.6)
- A Used model gets up to 2 main actions: Move, Shoot, Charge, Close Combat, Use Expertise (Search/Computers/Lockpick/Presence), Prepare (reaction stance), Throw. Quick Actions are bought with Action Points (AP) rolled on dice. No facing. (s2.6-2.7)
- Dice: one white d20 Skill Die (faces 2-10 plus AP, Critical Point and X auto-fail faces) and up to four colours of d12 Effect Dice: black Damage, green Accuracy (numeric modifier to the skill roll), yellow Armor Reduction, blue Special; a red die is used for armor; max 4 dice of one colour. Success = adjusted roll <= Skill Value; specialities give +2. Which effect dice roll depends on weapon and range band (melee/short/long). (s2.4-2.5; dice sheet)
- Range: colour-coded rulers instead of inches - dice sheet lists Black 12, Blue 10, Green 8, Red 6, Yellow 4, Orange 2 inches (corrected from the rendered sheet); Move distance is a ruler colour on the card; difficult terrain drops you one ruler colour. (dice sheet; s2.7)
- Cover: line from shooter base centre to target centre; each object fully crossed gives -2 to the attacker's skill, and the target's armor +1 once total. Area terrain has an exception. Own-side models never give cover. (s2.9)
- Damage: weapon base damage + damage icons, split into physical/energy/radiation; armor rating (physical/energy, roll armor die, reduced by Reduce-Armor icons) blocks; unblocked points become damage tokens; radiation flips tokens to a double-sided radiation side; model dies when damage >= its Health attribute. (s2.10-2.11)
- AI (solo/co-op): AI models carry a Objective (Defeat, Go To, Use, Protect) plus a Subject; each model's AI card holds a response matrix and target-priority list; Protect uses a Defend response. Scenarios set objectives and triggers that change them mid-game; AI force built from AI cards with standard equipment, extra caps spent on armor, mods, chems, food. (AI Handbook s1-4)
- Other card types: unit, weapon, armor/clothing, item/equipment, mods, perks, chems, leader and heroic cards, power armor, settlement sheet; points costs per unit/item (costs PDF). Terrain: LoS/LoC, difficult terrain, area terrain, buildings, elevation (s2.9, s2.7).
- Modes: Battle (points), Narrative/scenario, Settlement/campaign, AI, Co-op.
