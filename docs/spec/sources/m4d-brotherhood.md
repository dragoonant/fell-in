# M4d Brotherhood of Steel data sources
- Units: w9cards/unit.pdf pdf idx 54 (Goddard, Knight, Paladin) and idx 14 (Initiate, Knight), idx 57 (Field Scribe). Printed armor is '-' on all.
- Power armor: w9cards/pa.pdf pdf idx 17 (T-45, T-51, T-60). Card columns read as STR, END, AGI bonuses (green powered side), then armor
  phys/energy/rad with the +1 Strong bonus; yellow side has its own STR/AGI bonus and plain armor. T-45: green +2 STR +3 END -1 AGI, armor 2+1/2+1/4+1;
  yellow +2 STR -1 AGI, armor 3/2/4. T-51: green +2/+3, 3+1/2+1/4+1; yellow +2, 3/3/4. T-60f (idx 13) and the Frame not entered.
- Weapons: w9cards/weapon.pdf idx 7 (Machete), 8 (Baton), 26 (Power Fist), 48 (Machine Gun Turret Mk1; the card is a unit-like card: Per 4, health 5,
  armor 2/1/X, starts the round with 3 tokens). Gauss Rifle and Minigun (idx 17-18, 40-41) not entered: primed-weapon rules unclear.
- Costs: wave9_costs.pdf (Knight 53, Initiate 35, Paladin 77, Field Scribe 41, T-45 44, T-51 60, Machete 3, Power Fist 6, Baton 10, turret 10).
