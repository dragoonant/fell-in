## Themed ground tints (M5)
- No new downloads: the themed boards (suburbs, red-rocket, junkyard) reuse the CC0 Dry Ground 01 maps above and only multiply a theme colour over them (src/client/board/themes.ts). Roads, rubble fields and radioactive puddles are drawn procedurally (src/client/board/ProceduralTerrain.tsx).
